# Calendar System

## Structure

- 13 months, 28 days each = 364 days
- Every month is identical in length, so every date falls on the same weekday every year
- 1 additional New Year day outside the month structure, syncing the calendar to Earth's 365-day year
- In leap years, 2 New Year days instead of 1, celebrated as an extended, doubled festival

## Seasons

12 of the 13 months are grouped into 6 agricultural seasons of 2 months each:

1. Awakening
2. Planting
3. Growing
4. Ripening
5. Harvest
6. Dormant

The 13th month stands alone as a 7th, unpaired period: **Preparation**, immediately preceding the New Year festival. It mirrors real-world midwinter traditions, where celebrations were anchored near the solstice so people had something to anticipate during the hardest, darkest stretch — giving players a season built around stockpiling, gathering, and readying for the year ahead.

## Year-End Festival

- 1 day (non-leap years) or 2 days (leap years)
- The 2-day version is not just longer but a distinct, doubled celebration
- Falls immediately after Preparation, outside the 13-month structure entirely
